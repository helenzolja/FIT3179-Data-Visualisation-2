Diversity of Australian Flora

Australia is home to a remarkable diversity of native plant species, from flowering plants and ancient ferns to mosses, algae and gymnosperms. These groups contribute to the country's unique botanical heritage.

However, how does Australia's plant diversity compare with the rest of the world?

The visualisation compares the number of described plant species recorded in Australia against worldwide totals across five major plant groups.

Although worldwide species totals are much larger, comparing the groups reveals how Australia's contribution varies across different branches of plant diversity.
